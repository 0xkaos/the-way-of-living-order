# Version history

Numbered snapshots in `versions/` are authoritative. The unversioned files `the-way-of-living-order.md` and `the-way-of-living-order.docx` are retained as the original Version 1 working export; new revisions receive new numbered files.

## Version 9 — 12 September 2026

Combines Version 8's concise frame with selected evidence from Version 7.

- Restores a full four-test explanation of human rights, including the need for reciprocal obligation and sustaining institutions.
- Applies viability, truth, nesting, and posterity directly to Western failures without turning the section into a general civilizational history.
- Expands Japan only to a short, explicitly schematic case that the author can readily defend: prior capacity, deliberate learning, imperial capture, and postwar reconstruction.
- Replaces the stacked Japan references with one general Library of Congress history and marks the interpretation as non-specialist.
- Keeps the Constitution, American maintenance problem, and artificial-intelligence applications compact.
- States in the conclusion that comparative rankings are trial applications and that the path is not the property of a civilization.

## Version 8 — 12 September 2026

Returns the civilizational material to a succinct demonstration of the metric.

- Rebuilds from Version 6 rather than carrying forward Version 7's extended comparative analysis.
- Compresses Christianity, Judaism, other religions, and the Western claim into examples of later tools in the same living direction.
- Applies the metric briefly to human rights, the United States Constitution, Japan, American institutional maintenance, and artificial intelligence.
- Reduces Japan to one illustrative paragraph and removes the detailed Meiji, imperial, and postwar scorecard.
- Keeps the Western conclusion provisional without making civilizational ranking a major destination of the paper.
- Removes the paragraph explaining the author's purpose for developing a naturalistic account, keeping purpose separate from the paper's substantive claims.

## Version 7 — 12 September 2026

Makes civilizational history a fuller trial of the predefined living direction.

- Explains why cultural and institutional history should display a larger, noisier version of the same fight visible in organisms.
- Gives human rights a direct four-test evaluation as protection of viable, truthful, nested, and future agency.
- Applies the same criteria at comparable length to Western failures including slavery, empire, censorship, totalizing ideology, consumerism, and ecological exhaustion.
- Adds modern Japan as a full non-Western case: Meiji learning and reconstruction, imperial proxy capture and catastrophe, and postwar reconstruction through rights, public institutions, health, education, and industry.
- Treats the borrowing and adaptation of successful foreign tools as evidence that the direction is transferable rather than the property of a civilization.
- Retains the provisional Christian and Western rankings while making historical performance—not civilizational identity—the object being judged.

## Version 6 — 12 September 2026

Clarifies the changing epistemic status of the framework and its applications.

- Distinguishes the observable physical and biological foundation from the more interpretive extension of the metric to religions, civilizations, and institutions.
- States that no mature school has yet established agreed boundaries, weights, historical comparisons, or a body of scholarship around the proposed metric.
- Retains the substantive Christian and Western conclusions while presenting them as the author's current application: “when I apply the metric, this is how the comparison appears.”
- Describes the specialization account of American moral strain as one proposed explanation among many rather than a result isolated by survey evidence.
- Makes the comparative claims explicitly public, self-applying, provisional, and open to revision by better evidence.

## Version 5 — 12 September 2026

Rebuilds the paper around the simplified directional argument.

- Makes natural decay the source of resistance, the living fight the source of direction, selection the shaper of performance, and performance the basis of value.
- Removes the extended mathematical schema and worked-case catalogue from the main argument.
- Defines the individual's primary obligation as enacting the direction rather than possessing an abstract theory of it.
- Treats stories, traditions, and personifications as useful proxies while assigning greater epistemic responsibility to teachers, scholars, clergy, lawmakers, and system designers.
- Separates the observable scope of the “God of man” from the open question of a creator prior to the universe without introducing an irrelevant clockmaker contrast.
- Reorganizes Christianity, Judaism, other religions, and the Western canon as comparable maps of the same direction.
- Adds a qualified discussion of declining American religious affiliation, civic strain, specialization, and the moral conditions required by free institutions.
- Limits extended applications to the United States Constitution and artificial intelligence, and adds the perception of beauty as supporting evidence of an inherited orientation toward living form.

## Version 4 — 12 September 2026

Strengthens the treatment of one true way and religious convergence.

- Defines the one way as an objective direction of evaluation rather than a single rulebook or uniform civilization.
- Treats human map-making and correction of the good as characteristic of reflective human agency.
- Describes religions as high-value stores of moral knowledge that remain comparable by fidelity and formative results.
- Interprets cross-cultural convergence as the result of partly successful attempts to discover the same real order, without assuming equal accuracy.
- States the comparative achievement of the Western synthesis directly while making the judgment empirical, revisable, and non-ethnic.

## Version 2 — 11 September 2026

Refined from the fifth discussion.

- Clarifies that the forest, not raw fire or entropy production, carries reconstructive value; ecologically functional fire is evaluated within the larger living system.
- Treats the formal variables and horizon as bookkeeping devices rather than claimed laboratory measurements.
- Defines membership in the living field without making human worth depend on productivity.
- Adds a priority rule for conflicts among individual, collective, present, future, and ecological goods.
- Marks the limits and possible counterexamples in the comparison with Christianity.
- Adds comparative treatment of Islam, Taoism, Confucian thought, and the Enlightenment.
- Makes truthful pattern recognition and correction part of the obligation itself.
- Works through fertility versus consumption, open scientific AI versus closed proxy AI, and constitutional emergency power.
