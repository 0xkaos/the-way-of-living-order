**Updated thesis**

Life is the organization of matter into self-maintaining, self-repairing, reproducing form. That organization arose and was refined as dissipative structuring under continuous energy flux, followed by selection; the resulting design is the non-arbitrary product of those dynamics. Value is the degree to which any process, structure, or practice supports the continued capacity of organized systems to persist against decay. Ought appears only with human choice and marks a minimum threshold: the requirement to act so as to fulfill rather than deny the design’s conditions of viable organization. Actions above the threshold may increase value but are not obligatory; actions that drop below it constitute denial. The account is non-cosmological—it works solely from the design that exists and does not identify that design with any source prior to spacetime and causality. Existing biblical-to-legal frameworks already encode large parts of the same regularity; the lens clarifies the baseline they track and supplies a way to test drift, without generating a new orthodoxy.

**Integrated counters and scope**

Cancer (and similar short-term maximizers) fails the way because it abandons the cooperative constraints that sustain the larger organized system; both the local lineage and the host typically collapse. The entropy exchange that maintains form is local at each scale yet constitutive of larger systems (organism, group, institution). The trajectory therefore remains the reference at every level at which persistent organization is observed.

The thesis stays a baseline constraint. It does not prescribe a full moral code, revised canon, or institutional blueprint. It isolates the regularity visible in the design, notes that much of the inherited corpus already operates in rough accordance with it, and leaves further elaboration to collective work. The value gradient ranks states of affairs; the ought threshold keeps the demand finite and internal to human agents.


** Another version **

Life is the rare, cumulative process of self-maintaining patterns that locally resist entropy and decay. From its conditional origins onward, selection has favored variants better able to repair, persist, replicate, and process relevant information against the background tendency toward disorder. This trajectory—not any external supernatural agent—is the source of value: what extends ordered, information-rich persistence is good; what accelerates dissolution is not.The laws of matter and the pre-causal conditions that make any such process possible remain unknowable and bracketed. What is knowable and normative is the earth-bound path itself. Once organisms develop the capacity for choice—modeling alternatives and selecting among them—they acquire an obligation to continue and fulfill that path rather than betray it. Human beings, as the lineage in which this capacity is most developed, stand under that demand whether or not they acknowledge it.
