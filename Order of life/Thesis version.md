Life is the organization of matter into self-maintaining, self-repairing, reproducing form. That organization arose and was refined as dissipative structuring under continuous energy flux, followed by selection; the resulting design is the non-arbitrary product of those dynamics. Value is the degree to which any process, structure, or practice supports the continued capacity of organized systems to persist against decay. Ought appears only with human choice and marks a minimum threshold: the requirement to act so as to fulfill rather than deny the design’s conditions of viable organization. Actions above the threshold may increase value but are not obligatory; actions that drop below it constitute denial. The account is non-cosmological—it works solely from the design that exists and does not identify that design with any source prior to spacetime and causality. Existing biblical-to-legal frameworks already encode large parts of the same regularity; the lens clarifies the baseline they track and supplies a way to test drift, without generating a new orthodoxy.
