- Life is matter organized into self-maintaining, self-repairing, reproducing form.
- That organization began as dissipative structuring under energy flux (photon capture as a response that stabilized certain arrangements) and was refined by selection.
- The entire historical trajectory constitutes an earth-bound “God”—not the unknowable pre-causal ground, but the process that produced our design.
- The design is non-arbitrary (constrained outcome of the dynamics); human ought is the fulfillment of that design rather than an external imposition.
- The frame is strictly non-cosmological: the only given is the design we have; the options are fulfillment or denial.
- Value is the broad, comparative measure of how far any thing supports the trajectory (relative distance from functional disorder). Ought is a human-specific minimum threshold of accordance with the design. Exceeding the threshold is possible and unobjectionable; falling below it is the point of denial.
- Religious narratives, especially biblical ones, are proximal articulations of the same trajectory of organization, disruption, and re-alignment.
