Life is the rare, cumulative process of self-maintaining patterns that locally resist entropy and decay. From its conditional origins onward, selection has favored variants better able to repair, persist, replicate, and process relevant information against the background tendency toward disorder. This trajectory—not any external supernatural agent—is the source of value: what extends ordered, information-rich persistence is good; what accelerates dissolution is not.The laws of matter and the pre-causal conditions that make any such process possible remain unknowable and bracketed. What is knowable and normative is the earth-bound path itself. Once organisms develop the capacity for choice—modeling alternatives and selecting among them—they acquire an obligation to continue and fulfill that path rather than betray it. Human beings, as the lineage in which this capacity is most developed, stand under that demand whether or not they acknowledge it.
